When it comes to childcare, most parents consider two options: daycare or hiring a private nanny.

But there's a third option that many families don't discover until someone tells them about it — a nanny share.

What is a nanny share?
Two families share one nanny. You get personalized, in-home childcare at a fraction of the cost of a private nanny — and your child gets the benefits of a small, familiar environment with another child to grow alongside.

The idea is simple. The hard part has always been finding another family with a similar schedule, location, and childcare preferences.

FamLink is a platform built specifically for that. Browse Oakland families, compare schedules and preferences, and connect when you find the right fit.
